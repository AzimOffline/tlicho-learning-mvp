# Asset notes

- `public/tlicho-mark.svg`, `public/tlicho-course.svg`, `public/northern-hero.svg`, and `public/landing-landscape.svg` were created directly for this MVP. They are neutral geometric landscape illustrations and do not represent or claim a culturally significant Tłı̨chǫ symbol.
- The active mascot family in `public/characters/tactile/` was generated for this prototype with OpenAI's built-in image-generation workflow. The original set includes a moose, bison, caribou, red fox, beaver, and grizzly bear in a shared tactile 3D style. The moose also has purpose-made guide, reading, and sidebar-peek poses so repeated interface placements do not reuse one image. These are generic wildlife characters and do not represent or claim Tłı̨chǫ cultural symbols.
- `app/favicon.ico`, `app/icon1.png`, `app/icon2.png`, and `app/apple-icon.png` are cropped and resized derivatives of the active moose mascot so the browser tab and installed-app icon match the interface.
- The previous Kenney animal pack remains in `public/characters/kenney/` with its included CC0 license for provenance, but it is no longer used by the Tłı̨chǫ course or marketing page.
- Existing UI icons, sounds, and illustrations inherited from the original MIT-licensed clone remain in the repository where legacy screens still reference them.

Vocabulary and audio provenance is documented separately in `AUDIO_LICENSE_STATUS.md`.
